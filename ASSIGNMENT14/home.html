Chitkara University, established in 2002 by Dr. Ashok K. Chitkara and Dr. Madhu Chitkara, is a leading private institution in North India with campuses in Punjab and Himachal Pradesh. Recognized by the UGC and accredited with NAAC A+, it offers a wide range of programs in engineering, business, health sciences, law, hospitality, and design. Known for its strong industry collaborations, global partnerships with over 300 universities, and excellent placement records, the university provides modern infrastructure, research-driven learning, and a vibrant campus environment, making it one of the most reputed private universities in the region.
